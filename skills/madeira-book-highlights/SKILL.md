## How it works in the page (already built in)
- Each picture is a `PHOTOS['<storyId>:<index>']` entry in `index.html` (story ids and file names: Project facts). It holds `<defs>`, an `<image id="<p>ph">` at 465.8×260, the `<g class="life">…</g><!--/life-->` animation layer, a `dimmer` rect, then one `${o('<keys>',`…`)}` group per tappable thing (`<p>` = story id + index, e.g. `sabado3`).
- Words in the page text are written `[shown|key]`. Tapping a word adds `hl` to every group whose class includes `k-<key>`, and `focus` to the svg, for 2.6 s.
- **A region is a pixel mask, not a polygon.** Group markup:
  `${o('<keys>',`<use href="#<p>ph" mask="url(#<p>m<i>)"/><rect class="bb" x y width height fill="none"/>`)}`
  with, in `<defs>`: `<mask id="<p>m<i>" maskUnits="userSpaceOnUse" x y width height><image href="data:image/png;base64,…" x y width height preserveAspectRatio="none"/></mask>` — a small grey PNG (white = shown), cropped to the region, half master resolution, soft 1px edge, 16 grey levels. About 5 KB per region.
- **Inflate:** the invisible `rect.bb` is the region's bounding box; `inflateOrigin()` in the page script uses `rect.bb,polygon` to scale the group (1.03) around its own centre with a soft springy ease. The dimmer darkens everything else; `drop-shadow` gives the short warm glow pulse, which follows the cut-out's real edge.
- **Look-at beam:** a group containing `<g class="gaze">` draws a faint cone, a fine drifting dotted line and a soft ring from the looker's eyes to what is being looked at. It does not inflate, has no glow, and dims the picture only slightly (`.08`). Reduced-motion users get both effects without animation.

Keep these settings. If the user asks for stronger or subtler effects, change the CSS values, not the structure.

## Action words that aren't visibly happening (the user's rule)
If a word describes an action that the picture does not visibly show (waking up, opening, paying, climbing, ordering, drinking, the sun going down), **don't highlight it**. Instead:
- leave it without a region, and list it in the page's `NOT_SHOWN` const (`{"<storyId>:<index>": ["<key>", …]}`);
- record it in `gen/flagged_actions.json` as `{"<storyId>:<index>": {"<key>": "<what a future picture or small animation should show>"}}`, e.g. `"desce": "the sun sinking into the sea (animation: sun slowly lowering)"`;
- the write step skips flagged keys.
When a page is redrawn or animated so the action becomes visible, remove the flag and build the region. Objects, places, people and feelings shown in the picture are highlighted as usual.

## Method
Work in master pixels (the 1600×898 page image from `gen/masters/`, else the artifact image upscaled). Describe each region as shapes:
- `sam(box, pos_points, neg_points, pick)` — SAM with a box plus clicks on the object (pos) and on neighbours to exclude (neg); `pick='best'` (model score) or `'big'` (largest of the 3 proposals — big objects like a wall or bed). Drop specks smaller than 4% of the largest piece.
- `poly(...)`, `ell(cx,cy,rx,ry)` — drawn shapes, only for things without edges (sky in a window, a room, a street seen from far) and tiny things (sun, coins, steam, a pedal, a handshake, exhaust puffs). `('mask', array)` uses a ready-made mask (e.g. the lit windows for *luzes*).
- `sub(shape)` cuts something out (the cat off the wall, Rita out of the window); `post(shape)` adds back after the cuts (Rita back into the room).
- **Big areas = "everything except"**: sky, sea, room, forest, market, a kart track are the whole picture or a band **minus** the people and objects in front (sub with SAM) — never a straight line through a person or a frame. Things wholly inside an area stay only when they belong to it (the sun in the sky); people and animals are always cut out.
- **Same thing, same mask:** words that mean the same thing in the picture share one mask (`desce` = the sun; `cor laranja`/`cor rosa` = the sky; `praia` = sand ∪ sea; `fotografia telemovel` = the phone). In a spec, list every such key in the group's keys (`('sol desce', …)`, `('ceu corlaranja corrosa', …)`); a key left out of the spec gets no region and its word silently does nothing (book 4 page 6 after a redraw). When a page is redrawn, clear any `NOT_SHOWN` / `flagged_actions.json` entry it now shows.
- **Key = the word's key, exactly:** check each spec key against the page text's `[shown|key]` (e.g. `cansada`, not `cansadas`, when the text says *cansada*); a mismatched key leaves the word dead.
- **Same thing, same colour across pages:** if a prop (a leash, a bag) changes colour between pages, fix the picture first (`madeira-book-illustrations`), then cut it.
- People seen from behind in a car: cut the headrest out of the person (`sub`) and keep the face whole (`post` an ellipse if SAM drops a cheek or mouth).
- Feelings (`feliz`, `gosta`, `obrigada`, `cansadas`, `vermelha` for a blush, `sorri`): the face(s), SAM or an ellipse. Actions (`compra`, `come`, `bebe`) that are visibly happening: hands + object. Names: the whole person, hat to feet. A seat with people on it (`banco de trás`): the seat area minus only the people in front of it, keeping its passengers.
- Curtains and other things beside doors: cut only the fabric, never the door leaf, and `sub` a person standing in front.
- Re-cutting existing pages: for each old outline, SAM with its box + 3 interior points (distance-transform peaks); keep the proposal with best IoU against the outline; IoU < 0.5 → keep the outline and flag it for a hand fix.

Core helpers (numpy/cv2/PIL):
```python
def sam_mask(P, box, pos=(), neg=(), pick='best', ref=None):
    pts=list(pos)+list(neg); lab=[1]*len(pos)+[0]*len(neg)
    ms,sc,_=P.predict(point_coords=np.array(pts,float) if pts else None, point_labels=np.array(lab) if pts else None,
                       box=np.array(box,float), multimask_output=True)
    k=int(np.argmax([((m&ref).sum()/max(1,(m|ref).sum())) for m in ms])) if ref is not None else \
      int(np.argmax([m.sum() for m in ms])) if pick=='big' else int(np.argmax(sc))
    m=ms[k].astype(np.uint8); n,lb,st,_=cv2.connectedComponentsWithStats(m)
    if n>2: m=np.isin(lb,[i for i in range(1,n) if st[i,4]>0.04*st[1:,4].max()]).astype(np.uint8)
    return m
# region = OR(plus shapes) AND NOT dilate(sub shapes, 5px) OR post shapes; then close 5px, open 3px
def mask_png(m):  # -> base64 PNG, box in master px

    b=io.BytesIO(); im.save(b,'PNG',optimize=True); return base64.b64encode(b.getvalue()).decode(),(x0,y0,x1,y1)
# svg units: x*465.8/1600, y*260/898; rect.bb = the mask's tight bounding box
```

## Step 1 — list the words
Read the artifact and collect every `[shown|key]` key on the page (`pagesN` arrays). Each needs something visible. Several keys can share a region (`o('cafe bebe', …)`), and one region can be several shapes (both cones for `gelado dois`). Mark action words that aren't visibly happening as flagged (see above), and note which visible action words need an action cue (Step 5).

## Step 2 — prompt each region
Put a coordinate grid (every 100 master px) on the picture and choose boxes and points from it; for narrow things (window panes, belts, a mirror) use a 2× crop with a 20 px grid. Specs for a book live in `conv/hl_book<N>.py` as `SPEC={index:[(keys,[shapes])]}`, registered in `hlspecs.py` (import and `SPECS` entry) and in `hlmask.py`'s `BOOK` map. Prefer SAM for every person and object; drawn shapes only for edgeless areas and tiny things.

**Shape tips from books 13–14:** two keys can share one region by writing them together in the spec (`('francelho paranoar', [...])`, `('manta circulos', [...])`); thin dark things such as harness straps cut best as a colour mask inside a polygon (`('mask', array)`, book 14 `arnes`: pixels with value < 95 inside the person's outline); a large area (sea, field, cliffs) is a polygon minus the people and objects in it (`sub(...)`), and a wing or sail that SAM fills patchily is better taken from a bigger cut minus the parts below it. When a word's thing spans the scene (the horizon), a thin polygon band is fine.

## Step 3 — per-word review sheet (required)
For every word, render the picture darkened to 22% with only that word's mask at full brightness and its outline in red, tiles labelled `p<page> <keys> [flag]`, 4 per row (`hlmask.py sheet <story> [pages]`). Look at every tile. A selection passes only if it holds just its thing: no straight cuts through people or frames, no neighbours (the cat on the wall, people on the beach), no specks or holes, nothing missing (feet, hat, tail). Fix failures with better points and negatives, `sub`, a shared mask, or `pick='big'`, then re-check those tiles. Don't write (`hlmask.py apply <story>`) or publish until every tile passes.

## Step 4 — look-at beams
Add a beam only for words about looking or seeing (`olha`, `vê`, `olham`, `observa`…) **and** only when the picture clearly shows a looker and a target (Rita and the moon, the cat and the two women). For a beam word, use the beam **instead of** a region. One beam group per key — remove an old one before adding a new one.
```python
import math
def gaze(gid, key, eye, targets, day=False):   # svg coords; targets [(x, y, radius)]
    ex,ey=eye; parts=[]
    for j,(tx,ty,tr) in enumerate(targets):
        a=math.atan2(ty-ey,tx-ex); px,py=-math.sin(a),math.cos(a); w=tr*1.6
        cone=f'{ex:.1f},{ey:.1f} {tx+px*w:.1f},{ty+py*w:.1f} {tx-px*w:.1f},{ty-py*w:.1f}'
        parts.append(f'<linearGradient id="{gid}g{j}" gradientUnits="userSpaceOnUse" x1="{ex:.1f}" y1="{ey:.1f}" x2="{tx:.1f}" y2="{ty:.1f}">'
                     f'<stop offset="0" stop-color="#FFE2B8" stop-opacity=".05"/><stop offset="1" stop-color="#FFE2B8" stop-opacity=".55"/></linearGradient>'
                     f'<polygon class="gz-cone" points="{cone}" fill="url(#{gid}g{j})"/>'
                     f'<line class="gz-line" x1="{ex:.1f}" y1="{ey:.1f}" x2="{tx:.1f}" y2="{ty:.1f}"/>'
                     f'<circle class="gz-ring" cx="{tx:.1f}" cy="{ty:.1f}" r="{tr:.1f}"/>')
    parts.append(f'<circle class="gz-eye" cx="{ex:.1f}" cy="{ey:.1f}" r="2.2"/>')
    cls='gaze gz-day' if day else 'gaze'
    return "${o('" + key + "',`<g class=\"" + cls + "\">" + "".join(parts) + "</g>`)}"
```
- `eye`: between the looker's eyes, nudged slightly toward the target. `day=True` for bright daytime pictures (adds a faint shadow edge); night pictures use the plain version. Several targets are fine.
- If the CSS for `.gaze` is missing from the page, add it from the current artifact version; don't invent new values.

## Step 5 — action cues (the user's design: every action word, every book)
An action word that **is** visibly happening gets a cue on top of its normal highlight, so the reader sees that the word is something happening, not a thing. Tapping *gato* lights the cat; tapping *passa* makes it walk.
- **In the text:** action words get a **wavy underline** (`.w.act`; new = coloured wavy, known = faint wavy). The legend under the text has a third entry, "action" (`#lact`, `UI` string `lact` in all six languages).
- **In the picture**, by kind of action. Each entry lives in `gen/act.json` as `"<storyId>:<index>:<key>": {…}` (svg units, 465.8×260; master px × 0.2911 for x and × 0.2895 for y), and `conv/actfx.py` writes it into the page as `const ACT={…}`:
  - **Movement** (*passa, entram, nada, carregam, sobe, desce, corre, arrancam, ultrapassa, salta, mergulha*): `{"t":"move","dx":14,"dy":1,"len":26}`. The cut-out glides by (dx,dy) over 1.4 s (`.act-move`), three short speed lines appear behind it, and a small arrow draws on ahead of it. Optional `ox`/`oy` move the cue lines; `spread` sets the speed-line spacing; `box` overrides the bounding box; `"trail":false` drops the speed lines (for a mover heading toward the camera, *caminhar*, and for a word whose region is several far-apart movers, *arrancam*: the lines sit behind the combined box's centre and float in empty space otherwise). **Hide the original:** every move entry needs a clean plate, `"plate":"img/act/<storyId><index><key>.webp","pbox":[x,y,w,h]`. To make one:
    1. Run a Krea regional edit that removes only the mover ("Remove the tabby cat walking in the front left. Where it was, continue the black volcanic sand … as if the cat had never been there." + edit style sentence + "Keep everything else the same."). One removal edit can serve several movers on a page (book 13 page 5: all dolphins removed once, then `mkplate.py` for *nada* and *salta*).
    2. Crop the plate to the mover's mask, dilated 15 px and feathered (blur 5, ×1.3): `conv/mkplate.py <story> <index> <key> <plate.png>` writes `img/act/…webp`, prints `plate`/`pbox`, and saves a before/after preview to check (`gen/plates/prev_…jpg`). **The mover's region must hold everything that travels with it** (Rita's tote, the walkers' backpacks); otherwise that part stays behind as a ghost. Fix the region first, then re-cut and rebuild the plate.
    3. Save it as RGBA WebP.
    4. **When Krea won't remove a big mover** (two karts with drivers in the foreground, book 11 page 4: it removed only the small ones in the back, even on a crop): paint them out in code (`cv2.inpaint` TELEA radius 25 over the movers' mask dilated 21 px, then a blur 9 inside the mask to hide the smears), upload that smudged picture, and run a regional edit: "Repair the smudged, blurry patches on the race track: redraw them as clean, empty dark grey asphalt, continuing the white edge line and the red-and-white kerb smoothly through them … No karts, no people on the track." That gave a clean plate in one pass. One plate can serve several move words on a page (*arrancam* and *ultrapassa*); `mkplate.py` crops it to each word's own mask.
    5. The page inserts it **before the dimmer rect** while the mover glides, so the dim matches. The mover's shadow stays put; that is accepted.
  - **Movement shown by direction only** (falling water, the sun setting or rising, rain, a car climbing a far-off road, a sled being pushed, a paraglider rising, flying, landing, a bird circling, hoisting a sail, pointing): `{"t":"arrow","x1":186,"y1":40,"x2":186,"y2":110}`. An arrow draws on along the given line and nothing glides, so no plate is needed. Add `"pulse":true` to also squeeze the highlight (*empurram*). Keep the arrow beside what moves, not across it (book 14: the arrow for *sobe em círculos* first crossed the buzzard and was moved above it).
  - **Hands or body** (*abraça, segura, monta, pendura, brindam, arruma, aperta, mãos dadas, encosta, encolhe os ombros, amarra, inclina-se*): `{"t":"hold"}`. Two gentle squeeze-and-release pulses on the person and object (`.act-hold`).
  - **Sleeping or breathing** (*dorme, adormece, respira fundo*): `{"t":"rest"}`. One slow breathing swell (`.act-rest`).
  - **Spinning or turning round** (*dá um pião*): `{"t":"spin"}`. The cut-out twists −9° → 6° → −2° → 0 over 1.3 s (`.act-spin`; `actFx` treats it like hold/rest, `actClear` removes the class, reduced motion turns it off). No plate needed.
  - **Sound or expression** (*grita, ri, sorri, pisca o olho, canta, chama, bate palmas, acena*): `{"t":"sound","x":116,"y":100,"ang":-140,"spread":28,"r1":25,"r2":34}`. Three short cream lines with a dark edge flash from a point. Put them **beside the head, eye, clapping hands or waved object, pointing away from the face**, never across a face. For a shout, aim up-left or up-right of the head with r1 ≈ 25; for a wink or smile, r1 ≈ 6–7 beside the eye or the face's upper corner; for clapping, r1 ≈ 10 above the hands, pointing up.
  - Looking words keep their beam (above) and get no ACT entry. Actions that aren't visibly happening stay flagged and get no cue. States that aren't actions (*mora, gosta, tem*) get none either.

## Related gotcha: animation sprite sheets
Animation layers are sprite sheets shown through nested `<svg>` windows. Frames in a sheet need a ≥6 px transparent gutter between them, or the neighbouring frame bleeds in as a thin line at the window's edge. The page's sizing rule must stay `.pic svg:not(.life svg){width:min(100%,var(--picw,100%))…}` (the `:not(.life svg)` part) so it doesn't stretch those windows.
