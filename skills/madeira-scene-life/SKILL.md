# Histórias da Madeira — scene life (painted frames)

Use this when a page's picture should feel alive, for every new page or book, and when the user says an effect looks wrong. The art comes from `madeira-book-illustrations` and the tap regions from `madeira-book-highlights`; do the tap regions first.

**One technique.** Every effect is a loop of frames made from the page's own painting and played over it as a sprite sheet. Never draw particles, SVG turbulence or glows on top. A frame is made by:
1. **Moving the painting's own pixels** (ripple, sway, scroll, rings) — the default for anything that moves all the time.
2. **Animating a painted element** (a splash taken from the painting or a Krea variant) — scaled, moved and faded as one shape, for one-off events.
3. **Adjusting the painting's own light** (breathe, twinkle).

## The feel (the user's standing rules)
- **Simple and tied to the story.** Show what the page's words need ("the big splash and some water entering the pool"), nothing more. No extra incoming waves, no lingering spray, no second effects competing for attention. 1–3 effects per page.
- **Natural motion, not dissolves.** Never cross-dissolve or optical-flow morph between two different drawings for an event: it reads as water fading in place. Events move: a splash rises fast, hangs briefly, drops and is gone.
- **No bands, seams or halos.** Soft-edged masks only; never a straight band, a box edge, or a light rim around rocks. One continuous layer for a body of water rather than several overlapping ones.
- **Still things stay pixel-still:** faces, bodies, rocks, boats, buildings, animals, harbour walls. Clothes hems and hair tips may sway slightly.
- **Only real moving pixels move.** Masks come from SAM cut-outs and colour tests, minus people (SAM, dilated 15 px) and rocks.
- **Seamless loops.** Every periodic motion completes a whole number of cycles per loop.
- **Strength follows the story's weather:** calm page = ripples only; normal = small splash, thin trickles; storm = big splash, broad sheet over the rocks.
- **Respect reduced motion.** The page CSS hides `.life` under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the still picture must read on its own.

Never morph toward a Krea pose for wind: it goes rubbery. Never morph a pool to variants and back: ripples seem to retreat.

## Water masks
- Sea: SAM or colour seeds, minus people (dilated 15), minus rocks (`lum<60–70`, opened 5, dilated 3), minus boats, harbour walls and anything solid in the water. Leaks seen before: a waiter's shirt, clouds, an islet with a ship, rocks, blue jeans and denim shorts next to the sea, a blue-and-white striped top — subtract them.
- Feather edges with a 1–2 px Gaussian, never more; a wider blur shows the original foam as a halo.
- Cut people out with SAM shapes, never rectangles: a box exclusion leaves a visible still rectangle in the moving water (seen on book 9 before the fix). Feather the cut 3–4 px.
- Sea next to green headlands: subtract the land with a green colour test closed 25 px; then keep only big connected pieces.
- Things inside the water (cable car cabins, boats far out) are cut by colour inside a small box (red > blue, or dark), closed and dilated 5 px, never by the box itself.
- Check the mask in red over the picture before building.

## Splash-to-pool events
If the still picture already shows the splash (it usually does), the ripple layer needs a **calm plate**: take only water pixels from a Krea variant with the sea calm or retreating, keep the original rocks and people, and fill any rocks the variant added with its own water (normalized blur of its water pixels). Then:
- **Splash shape** = where the original is brighter and whiter than the calm plate, inside the sea, behind the front rocks. Anchor `cx` (median x) and `yb` (92nd percentile y).
- **Rise:** scale y from .05 to 1 (ease-out quad), x from .7 to 1, alpha ramps in over the first third; no pop.
- **Hang:** grow 4 %.
- **Drop:** fall 110 px with gravity, squash 25 %, light blur ≤2.6, alpha to 0 within ~1 s. Don't leave a mist cloud.
- **Trickles:** from a Krea "thin trickles between the rock tops" variant (keep only bright, low-saturation pixels that differ from the original, components > 25 px). Reveal top to bottom over ~1 s, streak texture scrolling down, fade by about half the loop.
- **Pool landing:** a little foam where the trickles land, spreading and fading (alpha ≤ .65).
- Rocks are excluded from the overlay alpha so the splash sits behind them.
```python
# per frame t in [0,1): composite with over(colour, alpha); keep alpha 0 on people and rocks
def warp(img,sx,sy,drop,ax,ay):
    return cv2.remap(img,(ax+(GX-ax)/sx).astype(np.float32),(ay+(GY-ay-drop)/sy).astype(np.float32),cv2.INTER_LINEAR)
r=seg(t,.05,.15); h=seg(t,.15,.20); f=seg(t,.20,.32)
p=1-(1-r)**2; sy=(.05+.95*p)*(1+.04*h)*(1-.25*f); sx=(.70+.30*p)*(1+.04*h+.12*f); drop=110*f*f
a=warp(splashAlpha,sx,sy,drop,cx,yb)*min(1,r*3)*(1-ease(f))**1.5; c=warp(B,sx,sy,drop,cx,yb)
```

## Helpers (`frames.py`)
`frames.py` holds `load, rgb, lum, sat, poly_mask, flow, warp, ripple, sway, fall_scroll, rings, steam, breathe, twinkle, save, loop, insert`; `windfx.add_layer(key, html, stem)` replaces a page's layer (empty html removes it). The core ones:
```python
def ripple(B,mask,n=16,near=3.2,far=.35,lam_near=70.,lam_far=12.,shine=.05,edge=8):
    m=(mask>.3).astype(np.uint8); ys=np.nonzero(m)[0]; y0,y1=ys.min(),ys.max()
    t=np.clip((GY-y0)/max(1,y1-y0),0,1); amp=far+(near-far)*t**1.3; lam=lam_far+(lam_near-lam_far)*t
    col=np.clip((np.arange(H,dtype=np.float32)-y0)/max(1,y1-y0),0,1); lam_y=lam_far+(lam_near-lam_far)*col
    Phi=np.cumsum(2*np.pi/lam_y)[:,None].astype(np.float32)*np.ones((1,W),np.float32)
    fade=np.clip(cv2.distanceTransform(m,cv2.DIST_L2,5)/edge,0,1); amp=amp*fade; out=[]
    for i in range(n):
        s=i/n; p1=Phi+.6*np.sin(2*np.pi*GX/(3.1*lam))-2*np.pi*s; p2=.8*Phi+2*np.pi*GX/(2.3*lam)-4*np.pi*s
        dy=amp*(.65*np.sin(p1)+.35*np.sin(p2)); dx=amp*.45*np.cos(p2)
        f=cv2.remap(B,(GX+dx).astype(np.float32),(GY+dy).astype(np.float32),cv2.INTER_CUBIC,borderMode=cv2.BORDER_REFLECT)
        out.append(np.clip(f*(1+shine*fade[...,None]*(.4+.6*t[...,None])*np.sin(p1)[...,None]),0,255))
    return out
def sway(B,w,n=16,amp=2.5,lam=260.,gust=.35,vertical=.25):   # w = per-pixel weight 0..1, faces 0
    out=[]
    for i in range(n):
        s=i/n; g=np.sin(2*np.pi*(s-GX/lam))+gust*np.sin(2*np.pi*(2*s-GX/(lam*.45)+.3))
        out.append(cv2.remap(B,(GX-amp*w*g).astype(np.float32),(GY-amp*vertical*w*np.sin(2*np.pi*(s-GX/lam)+1.2)).astype(np.float32),cv2.INTER_CUBIC,borderMode=cv2.BORDER_REFLECT))
    return out
def fall_scroll(B,n=24,P=70.,direction=(0,1)):   # two copies half a cycle apart, cross-weighted
    ux,uy=direction; L=(ux*ux+uy*uy)**.5; ux,uy=ux/L,uy/L; out=[]
    for i in range(n):
        ta=i/n; tb=(ta+.5)%1; wa=1-abs(2*ta-1); wb=1-abs(2*tb-1)
        out.append((wa*shift(B,ux*ta*P,uy*ta*P)+wb*shift(B,ux*tb*P,uy*tb*P))/(wa+wb))
    return out
def breathe(B,g,n=16,amp=.08): return [B*(1+amp*.5*(1-np.cos(2*np.pi*i/n))*g)[...,None] for i in range(n)]
def streak_tile(T,count,length,width,slope,seed):   # rain: one seamless tile of anti-aliased streaks
    rng=np.random.default_rng(seed); t=np.zeros((T*3,T*3),np.float32)
    for _ in range(count):
        x,y=rng.uniform(0,T,2); L=length*rng.uniform(.6,1.2); a=rng.uniform(.5,1)
        for ox in (0,T,2*T):
            for oy in (0,T,2*T): cv2.line(t,(int(x+ox),int(y+oy)),(int(x+ox+slope*L),int(y+oy+L)),a,width,cv2.LINE_AA)
    return cv2.GaussianBlur(t[T:2*T,T:2*T],(0,0),.6)
def rain_layer(n,layers,keep):   # alpha frames; each layer falls a whole number of tiles per loop
    out=[]
    for k in range(n):
        A=np.zeros((H,W),np.float32)
        for T,count,length,width,slope,tiles,alpha,seed in layers:
            big=np.tile(streak_tile(T,count,length,width,slope,seed),(H//T+2,W//T+2)); dy=tiles*T*k/n
            A=np.maximum(A,big[int(round(-dy))%T:,int(round(-slope*dy))%T:][:H,:W]*alpha)
        out.append(A*keep)
    return out   # save as RGBA: constant colour (232,238,245) with these alphas
def rain_rings(B,pud,n=24,drops=55,Rm=30,amp=3.2,lam=6.,sq=.42,seed=3):   # rings bend the painted puddle, plus light
    rng=np.random.default_rng(seed); ys,xs=np.nonzero(pud); k=rng.choice(len(xs),drops,replace=False); ph=rng.uniform(0,1,drops); out=[]
    for f in range(n):
        dx=np.zeros((H,W),np.float32); dy=dx.copy(); lit=dx.copy()
        for x,y,p in zip(xs[k],ys[k],ph):
            age=((f/n)+p)%1; r=Rm*age; a=amp*(1-age)**1.4
            x0,x1,y0,y1=max(0,x-Rm-8),min(W,x+Rm+8),max(0,int(y-Rm*sq)-8),min(H,int(y+Rm*sq)+8)
            gx,gy=GX[y0:y1,x0:x1]-x,(GY[y0:y1,x0:x1]-y)/sq; rr=np.sqrt(gx*gx+gy*gy)+1e-3
            band=np.exp(-((rr-r)/lam)**2); s=a*band*np.sin(2*np.pi*(rr-r)/lam)
            dx[y0:y1,x0:x1]+=s*gx/rr; dy[y0:y1,x0:x1]+=s*gy/rr*sq; lit[y0:y1,x0:x1]+=band*(1-age)**1.2*.45
            if age<.18: lit[y0:y1,x0:x1]+=np.exp(-(gx**2+(gy*sq)**2)/(2*3.5**2))*(1-age/.18)*1.2
        g=cv2.remap(B,GX+dx,GY+dy,cv2.INTER_CUBIC,borderMode=cv2.BORDER_REFLECT); out.append(np.clip(g+(255-g)*np.clip(lit,0,1)[...,None]*.8,0,255))
    return out
def waterfall(B,m,n=24,P=240,seed=11):   # streaks in the fall's own light colour; mask = the fall's SAM cut-out
    m=(m>0).astype(np.uint8); ys,xs=np.nonzero(m); y0,y1=ys.min(),ys.max(); U=48
    noise=np.random.default_rng(seed).random((P,U)).astype(np.float32)
    tex=cv2.GaussianBlur(np.concatenate([noise]*3,0),(0,0),sigmaX=1.1,sigmaY=9)[P:2*P]; tex=(tex-tex.mean())/tex.std()
    span={y:(np.nonzero(m[y])[0].min(),np.nonzero(m[y])[0].max()+1) for y in range(y0,y1+1) if m[y].any()}
    col=np.percentile(B[m>0],85,axis=0); sm=np.clip(cv2.GaussianBlur(m.astype(np.float32),(0,0),1.3),0,1); out=[]
    for k in range(n):
        a=np.zeros((H,W),np.float32)
        for y,(xa,xb) in span.items():
            t=(y-y0)/max(1,y1-y0); v=(y-y0)*(1+.8*t)-k*P/n*(1+.6*t)     # accelerating fall
            a[y,xa:xb]=np.interp(np.linspace(0,U-1,xb-xa),np.arange(U),tex[int(np.floor(v))%P])
        a=np.clip(a*.5+.15,-.6,1.2)*sm; lt=np.clip(a,0,None)[...,None]*.55; dk=np.clip(-a,0,None)[...,None]*.18
        f=(B*(1-lt)+col*lt)*(1-dk)
        mist=np.zeros((H,W),np.float32); cv2.ellipse(mist,(int(xs[ys>y1-4].mean()),y1+4),(34,14),0,0,360,1.,-1); mist=cv2.GaussianBlur(mist,(0,0),7)
        pl=.28+.12*np.sin(4*np.pi*k/n); out.append(f*(1-mist[...,None]*pl)+np.float32([248,248,244])*mist[...,None]*pl)
    return out   # layer mask = the fall (soft) plus an ellipse of spray at its foot
```
`save(frames,mask,base,name)` writes RGBA WebPs at 0.75 scale cropped to the mask box; `loop(paths,box,dur)` writes the frames into the page; then run `repack.py` (packs every frame set into one `<stem>_sheet.webp`, shown through a nested `<svg viewBox>` window with a discrete translate plus an opacity crossfade to the next frame) and `regutter.py` (6 px transparent gutter between frames; without it the neighbouring frame bleeds in as a thin line). Page CSS must keep `.pic svg:not(.life svg){width:min(100%,var(--picw,100%))…}` (the fit-to-screen reader sets `--picw`) so the windows aren't stretched. Keep sheets under 16 000 px a side. A book's effects can live in one script (`b<N>life.py <page|all> [preview]`): `preview` writes each mask in red over the picture to look at before building.

## Building a page
1. **Look.** Find `PHOTOS['<storyId>:<index>']` (story ids: Project facts). Read the page text: what must visibly happen? Pick effects from the table.
2. **Masks** from SAM and colour; draw them in red and look.
3. **Frames** with the method; for events, a review strip of every 2nd frame at 2× zoom around the action.
4. **Insert** with `loop`, removing that page's old layers for the same thing, then repack and regutter. Delete retired sheets by publishing them as `null`.
5. If an action word in the text isn't visibly happening, either animate it (small and clear) or leave it flagged in `gen/flagged_actions.json` / `NOT_SHOWN` (see `madeira-book-highlights`). Action words that are happening get an action cue on tap (`madeira-book-highlights`, "Action cues"); scene life and cues are separate layers, so keep both.

## Checks
0. **Preview first** when reworking an effect the user flagged: a short MP4 (`imageio`, 1200 px wide, 2–3 loops) and a still strip; publish after they approve.
1. **Step sizes:** mean change between consecutive frames stays even through the loop, including last→first (no jump at the seam).
2. **Heat map / motion map** of the max change (in the browser: 10 screenshots of `#pic` 450 ms apart with the page's slow zoom paused, or `heatone.py <out.jpg> <story:index>…` from the sheets): only water, hair, leaves, flags, smoke or rain light up; rocks, faces, boats and walls stay black. White rain over a white sky barely shows on the map; that is fine.
3. **Zoom** (2×) on rock edges, the harbour and the pool rim in the calmest and busiest frames: no halos, smears, bands or ghost rocks.
4. **Browser:** open the page in Playwright (Chromium at `/opt/pw-browsers`), screenshot `#pic` several times across one loop; the motion plays and the sheet windows show one frame.
5. **Syntax and size:** `node --check` on extracted scripts; note the sheet size (aim ≤ 1–1.5 MB per effect).
6. **Publish:** run `split.py` and publish `dist/index.html` with the new sheets in `files`, retired ones as `null` (Project facts, "Publishing"), then tell the user in plain words what moves and how long the loop is.
