## Picture plan
1. Sunny Paul da Serra plateau above a sea of clouds, sheep grazing; Rita's blue car beside Tiago's van; Bruno handing out wetsuits and helmets.
2. Group in wetsuits/helmets at the Dona Beja pool where the stream begins; Bruno clips a carabiner to Rita's harness; orange whistles on cords.
3. Rita abseiling backwards down a small waterfall, leaning back, feet flat on the rock, legs shaking; Tiago waist-deep below, arms open.
4. Mossy stone chute: Ana sliding into a pool, shouting; João diving after her; Rita laughing on the bank.
5. Sandwiches under laurel trees among ferns; a Madeira laurel pigeon (pombo-trocaz) flapping its wings, natural size.
6. High rock over a deep dark pool; Bruno mid-jump; Rita peering from the edge.
7. Tiago back on the rock reaching out his hand; then both splashing in holding hands, Rita laughing.
8. Late light at the cars, everyone in towels coiling ropes; Bruno talking; Tiago looking only at Rita.

## Outfits
Black canyoning wetsuits, harness, gloves, boots. Helmets: Rita white, Ana yellow, João blue, Tiago red, Bruno red with an orange guide's bag. Page 8: towels, everyday clothes in the cars.

## New character
Bruno — about 35, shaved head, broad shoulders, dark stubble, warm laugh; firefighter (bombeiro) who guides canyoning at weekends. João's friend.

Setting: Ribeira do Lajeado (beginner canyon, laurel forest, Rabaçal; starts at Lagoa Dona Beja by the Levada do Alecrim).
