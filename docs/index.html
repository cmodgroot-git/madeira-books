      • Sea scenes: Deep Atlantic and ocean shallows as the base, whitewash for foam and buildings, a small hit of bird-of-paradise orange.
      • Mountain and forest scenes: laurel forest, levada moss and Pico mist, with basalt for cliffs and paths.
      • Town scenes (Funchal, Santana): whitewash walls, terracotta roofs and basalt cobbles, with jacaranda or hydrangea for flowers.
      • Use basalt rather than pure black for outlines and text.
